Hermann Ebbinghaus measured his own memory for lists of nonsense syllables and published the result in 1885. Most of the loss happened in the first hours. After that the curve flattened: what survived the first day faded slowly.

Two things I take from it:

1. The first review matters most, and it should come soon.
2. Each review makes the curve flatter, which is why [[Spaced repetition]] works.

His material was meaningless on purpose. Things that connect to what I already know fade more slowly, so the exact numbers do not carry over to real studying.
