German for "slip box". The sociologist Niklas Luhmann kept about 90,000 paper slips this way and credited the box for much of what he wrote.

What made it work:

- **One idea per note.** A note should make sense on its own.
- **My own words.** Copying a quote does not count as a note.
- **Links, not folders.** A note belongs wherever it is linked from, so it can live in many contexts at once.

The links are where new ideas come from. Following them, I run into notes I had forgotten, next to the one I am working on.

This site is built the same way: see [[How this second brain works]]. A related idea is [[Evergreen notes]], notes written to be kept and improved over years.
