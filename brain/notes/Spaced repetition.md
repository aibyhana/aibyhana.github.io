Reviewing a fact several times with growing gaps in between keeps it for much longer than reviewing it the same number of times in one sitting.

The idea follows from the [[Forgetting curve]]: memory fades fast at first, and each review slows the fading. So the next review can wait longer than the last one did.

## In practice

- First review after a day, then about three days, a week, two weeks, a month.
- If I fail a review, the gap goes back to short.
- The review has to be [[Active recall]], not rereading. Looking at the answer does very little.

Flashcard programs such as Anki work out the gaps for me. The hard part is writing cards that ask one clear question.
