A way to find out whether I understand something, named after the physicist Richard Feynman.

1. Write the name of the idea at the top of a page.
2. Explain it in plain words, as if to someone who has never heard of it.
3. Notice where I get stuck, wave my hands, or fall back on jargon.
4. Go back to the source for exactly those parts, then explain again.

Step three is the point. A gap that stays hidden while I read shows up at once when I have to say it myself, which makes this a form of [[Active recall]].

Writing a note for this site is the same exercise.
