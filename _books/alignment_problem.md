A comprehensive exploration of AI alignment challenges, from reward hacking in reinforcement learning to value alignment and interpretability. Christian does an excellent job bridging technical concepts with real-world implications, making it essential reading for anyone interested in AI safety. The book covers fundamental problems in getting AI systems to do what we actually want them to do, rather than gaming their reward functions in unexpected ways.

Particularly relevant sections on interpretability methods and the challenges of ensuring AI systems remain aligned with human values as they become more capable. The historical context of how researchers discovered these problems provides valuable perspective on the field's evolution.
