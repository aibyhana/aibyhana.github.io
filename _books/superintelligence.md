Bostrom's foundational work on AI safety that laid out many of the core concerns about advanced AI systems. While some predictions about AI development timelines may need updating, the fundamental arguments about control problems, value alignment, and existential risk remain highly relevant.

The book systematically explores different paths to superintelligence and their implications, along with various proposed solutions to the alignment problem. Essential reading for understanding the theoretical foundations of AI safety, though it should be complemented with more recent empirical work on current LLM systems.
