From S. Keshav's short article "How to Read a Paper" (2007). The mistake is to start at the first word and read to the last.

| Pass | Time | What I read | What I get |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | 5 to 10 minutes | Title, abstract, introduction, headings, conclusion | What kind of paper it is, and whether to go on |
| Second | Up to an hour | Everything except proofs, with care for the figures | The argument, well enough to explain it to someone |
| Third | Hours | Every step, rebuilding the work in my head | The assumptions, the weak spots, what I would do differently |

Most papers only deserve the first pass.

After the second pass I write a note in my own words and link it, as in a [[Zettelkasten]]. If I cannot do that from memory, I have not read it yet: see [[Active recall]].
